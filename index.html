Privacy Policy

Last updated: —

Gift Shopping ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application Gift Shopping. By using the app, you agree to the terms of this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We want to assure you that Gift Shopping does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or payment details. The app only processes information that you choose to enter for your own use, including:

  • Budget amounts you set.
  • Item names, quantities, and prices.
  • Currency preference.

All of this data remains on your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you enter is used solely within the app to help you:

  • Track your shopping budget.
  • Manage and calculate your items and expenses.
  • View your shopping history (stored locally on your device).

We do not sell, share, or use your information for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

All information is stored locally on your device. We do not collect analytics or use third-party trackers. If you uninstall the app, the data stored by the app on your device will be removed according to the device's app removal behavior.

4. Third-Party Services

Gift Shopping does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising networks, or social platforms. If this changes, we will update this policy and notify users by updating the Effective Date.

5. Children's Privacy

Gift Shopping is safe for all users and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

6. Your Rights

Because we do not collect personal data, there is no account- or server-stored personal information to request access to, correct, or delete. You have full control of your information directly within the app.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected by an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the policy. Continued use of the app after the Effective Date constitutes acceptance of the updated terms.
